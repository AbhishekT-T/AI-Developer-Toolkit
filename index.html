Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Local In-Browser Processing

Resume parsing, keyword extraction, and score calculations run directly in your web browser. We do not transmit or retain copies of your uploaded resumes on permanent databases.

2. Rate Limiting

Anonymous rate limiting is enforced by hashing client IP addresses with the current date string (YYYY-MM-DD) in ephemeral memory to prevent automated abuse without tracking browsing history.

3. AI API Requests

When you trigger an AI feature, only the required text snippet is forwarded via SSL encryption to the AI provider endpoint under enterprise privacy terms.

4. Advertising & Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies such as Google AdSense to serve ads when you visit our website. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on user visits to this or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
